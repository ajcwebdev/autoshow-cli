A HISTORY OF NAVIGATION                                        41

The Long Voyages

For three generations the captains of Lisbon pressed steadily
southward along the African coast, each expedition carrying its
charts a little farther than the last. What had begun as a search for
gold and slaves became, almost by accident, a systematic survey of an
unknown shore.
