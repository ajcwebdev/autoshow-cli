Instruments of the Period

The pilots of the age relied on a small number of instru ments, each
suited to a different prob1em of position-finding. The principal tools
are summarized below.

Instrument    Measures        Introduced
Astrolabe     Latitude        c. 1480
Cross-staff   Star altitude   c. 1515
Log and line  Speed           c. 1570

Among these, three deserves particular mention:

- the astrolabe, for latitude at noon
- the cross-staff, for altitude of stars
- the log and line, for reckoning of speed

These were rarely accu-
rate, but in skilled hands they were enough.
