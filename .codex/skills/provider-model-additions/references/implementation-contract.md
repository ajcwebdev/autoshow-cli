# Standard Implementation Contract

Every actionable finding inherits these requirements. Its **Work** line names only the change that sets its class and anything that differs from or adds to this list. Meeting the contract is an implementation prerequisite; it is neither permission for paid execution nor evidence of live compatibility.

- **Registry and selectors:** add the record to the workflow registry under `src/cli/commands/setup-and-utilities/models/` and to the supported-model array in the workflow's `*-models.ts`. `supported-model-sources.ts` and `supported-model-source-contracts.test.ts` require the arrays and registry to agree in both directions.
- **Paid provenance:** each new paid record carries `pricingSourceUrl`, `pricingCheckedAt`, `pricingCurrency`, `pricingTier` and `pricingNotes` from the finding's pricing evidence. Unknown values stay explicitly unresolved; borrowed timing or token heuristics carry provisional `estimation.timingProvenance`.
- **Defaults and expansion.** Check the bare-provider default with `cheapest-models.ts`. It selects the cheapest default-eligible active model unless a documented policy pins a representative. `--all-*` includes expansion-eligible entries in registry order. Record any change to the default and pin it with a local test. [ADR-010](../../../../docs/adr/ADR-010-hosted-model-registry-lifecycle-and-capability-policy.md)
- **Lifecycle and identity:** a superseded ID leaves selection, defaults, help and expansion, and gains rate and same-service replacement entries in `model-loader/retired-model-rates.ts`, so finished runs keep their identity and historical rates, errors name the successor, and unfinished targets cannot resume under the old name. Coexisting tiers need a recorded trade-off.
- **Help, guides and links:** help and examples derive from the registries ([ADR-018](../../../../docs/adr/ADR-018-derive-cli-help-from-registries-and-generalize-provider-flags.md)). Update the workflow overview under `docs/commands/` and the provider link file under `src/cli/commands/setup-and-utilities/links/model-links/`; `check:docs` validates maintained examples against registered models.
- **Price preflight:** add mapped price-only commands for each new selector under `test/test-runner/price-commands/registry/`.
- **Local no-cost tests:** accepted controls through actual dispatch with a mocked transport; the bare-provider default, an explicit override and the reset path; rejection of unsupported models or controls before pricing, credentials and dispatch; estimator results including bands, tiers and minimums; valid and invalid response artifacts; and retired-name and resume recovery. Mocks establish request shape and decoding only, not text or spoken correctness, perceptual quality or provider acceptance.
- **Evidence order:** follow [ADR-015](../../../../docs/adr/ADR-015-govern-documentation-examples-and-verification-evidence.md): primary-source identity, then registry contracts, guides and local tests, then an exact `--price` preflight, and only then a live call with an estimate and approval under the repository's paid-provider rules.
- **TTS specifics:** keep native dialogue distinct from single-voice controls, inline delivery tags from request-level instructions, transport success from decoded integrity, and spoken correctness from perceptual quality. Inspect serializers, multi-speaker capability and voice provisioning before proposing a new control.
