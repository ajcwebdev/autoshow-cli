## Episode Summary

In this guest-free FS Jam episode, the Everfund founder and co-host Anthony discuss the little-documented craft of building third-party JavaScript scripts and widgets. Drawing on his work on Everfund's open-source donation SDK, the founder recounts the confusion around module formats (CommonJS, ESM, UMD) and why UMD is still needed for script loaders and WordPress sites. He explains why he combined Microbundle with Rollup to produce UMD builds and weighs ESM's roughly 93% browser support against the need for scripts to work everywhere. The pair compare hosting on private S3 buckets with npm CDNs, npm installs with script tags, and separate JavaScript and React SDKs, along with the trade-offs of iframes versus DOM injection: fewer CORS problems and easier updates, but limited CSS customization. He describes Everfund's move toward a headless donation system and a strange WordPress bug fixed by hosting the script locally. The conversation then shifts to documentation (the Divio framework, ReadMe, Stripe as the gold standard), their upcoming trip to Remix Conference, and plans for sponsorships and merchandise to make the podcast sustainable. They also discuss responsible developer advocacy and vetting guests, Anthony renounces Qovery, and both hosts name favorite recent episodes, including Hydrogen, Flight Control, Kent C. Dodds, Orta, and Miško Hevery.
