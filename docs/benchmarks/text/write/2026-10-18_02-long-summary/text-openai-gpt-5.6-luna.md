## Episode Summary

This conversational FS Jam episode explores the practical challenges of building third-party JavaScript scripts that work across websites, frameworks, browsers, and platforms such as WordPress. The hosts discuss bundling TypeScript or JavaScript into CommonJS, ESM, and especially UMD, weighing modern browser support against the need for reliable compatibility and smaller bundles. They compare npm packages, CDN-hosted scripts, and private or open-source distribution, then examine whether embedded UI should be rendered directly in the host DOM or inside an iframe, balancing customization and CSS access against isolation, CORS concerns, and easier updates. Everfund’s experience leads to a broader discussion of SDK design, React-specific integrations, headless functionality, and the difficulty of creating a universal abstraction. The conversation also touches on documenting APIs effectively, using tutorials, how-to guides, explanations, and references. Finally, the hosts discuss podcast guests, responsible product advocacy, vetting sponsors, merchandise, compensation, and their goal of making FS Jam sustainable while preserving useful long-term technical context.
