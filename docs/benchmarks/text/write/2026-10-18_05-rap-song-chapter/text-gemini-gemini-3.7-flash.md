# Habit of the Coast

Intro

Dawn across the harbor sill, ridge beneath a band of white.
Teal enamel mug beside the paper, pressed to keep it tight.
Cream compass on the glaze facing out the window frame.
Clean page waiting for the campsite name.

Verse 1

Papa Bear reviewed the sky, wrapped in coat of rusted wool,
Patient human field guide who never let the pacing pull
Past the margins of the day, checking weather, route, and pack.
Both of us held copies of the path in case we turned back.
I secured the water flask, spare cloth, torch, and binder clip,
Fastened down the notebook pages so the breeze could never slip.
At the trailhead post I took a picture of the guideboard map,
Weighed it with the printout, stepped across the timber gap.
Never took the gravel shortcut or the brighter hillside track;
Kept along the marked ascent where shelter kept the wind at back.
Paused beside the harbor overlook to trace the footbridge line:
Short along the waterline, long across the ridge incline.
Penciled both perspectives on the sheet without a claim of wrong,
Noting where the observer stood to watch the harbor draw along.

Chorus

Anchor down the paper while the harbor breezes blow.
Keep the compass steady where the marked trail markers show.
Draw the angle where you stand, let the quiet question grow:
Describe the evidence before you claim to know.

Verse 2

Pitch the shelter on the leveled ground and keep the path clear,
Tripod balanced on its feet before the shadows turn near.
Kept the optic in the case until the fading sunset died;
Lowered down the lantern flame and turned the metal shield aside.
Just a circle of warm light to find the mug and graphite lead,
Letting our adjusted sight receive the ridge where darkness spread.
Thin cloud cut the first star, masking what I hoped to trace;
Papa Bear said note the cloud as part of what was taking place.
Logged the pale wash overhead, the water breeze, the single spark,
Recording what was verified before the valley turned dark.
Trading stations at the lens without a guess of depth or span,
Noticing the harbor lamps grow sharper as the night began,
Cloud drift leaning to the north, pale band sinking down the crest,
Setting down the changing state before we offered any guess.

Chorus

Anchor down the paper while the harbor breezes blow.
Shield the lantern yellow so the distant lanterns show.
Draw the angle where you stand, let the patient silence grow:
Describe the evidence before you claim to know.

Verse 3

Sudden streak of burning white crossed the open zenith dome,
Gone before the pencil tip could track the line and bring it home.
Neither of us claimed the arc or called it what we could not prove;
Noted down the rough direction, time, and how it seemed to move.
Room remained for what was brief without an exaggerated tale;
Hands around the enamel rim until the tea turned pale.
Waiting brought a clearer sight than rushing out to name the sky.
Morning checklist on the board before the daylight mounted high:
Mug and notebook, lantern, clip, and optic case accounted for,
Not a scrap of paper left across the campsite gravel floor.
Down the same designated track until the bridge appeared in sight:
Drew a third small rendering beneath the early morning light.
Angles shifting with the sun without a line to be erased,
Holding every vantage true to where the eye was placed.

Bridge

Four clean pages gathered up, three sketches of the span,
Written so an absent hand could read the route we ran.
Every observation bound to station, time, and sight,
Leaving no debris behind, returning by first light.

Chorus

Teal mug resting on the sill where harbor breezes blow.
Cream badge facing to the sea, three honest sketches show.
Trust the angle where you stood, watch the careful habit grow:
Describe the evidence before you claim to know.
