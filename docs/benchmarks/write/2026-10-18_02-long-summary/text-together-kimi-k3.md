## Episode Summary

In this guest-free episode, the hosts dig into the surprisingly messy world of building third-party JavaScript widgets, drawing on Christopher's experience creating Everfund's open-source donation SDK. They cover the practical headaches: choosing between UMD, CommonJS, and ESM module formats, why UMD remains necessary despite ESM's roughly 93% browser support, bundling quirks that required pairing Microbundle with Rollup, and the trade-offs between iframe-based widgets and direct DOM injection around CORS, CSS encapsulation, and baffling WordPress edge cases. The conversation then turns to writing good documentation via the Divio framework (tutorials, how-tos, reference, and explanation) and to the podcast's own future: more co-host-only episodes, introducing sponsorships to make the show sustainable, vetting guests, and the ethics of developer advocacy and product endorsements. They wrap up with favorite recent episodes (Hydrogen, Flight Control, Kent C. Dodds, Orta, and Miško on Qwik), their upcoming trip to Remix Conference in Utah, and their broader aim of creating a lasting historical record of web development for future listeners.
