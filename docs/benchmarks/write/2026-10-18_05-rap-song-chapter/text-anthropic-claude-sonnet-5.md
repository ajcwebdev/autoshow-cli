# Weight on the Map

Intro

Mug on the table, teal shell holding the map flat,
Window open, ridge wrapped in mist, nothing certain but that.
Papa Bear reads the trail notice twice before we pack,
We write down the route so we know how to get back.

Verse 1

Torch, water, notebook, spare layer — I check it twice,
Papa Bear checks weather, trail, and the hour we're due home.
Shortcut gleams on the hillside, bright patch calling my name,
We stay on the marked path — the map doesn't change.
At the harbor overlook he shows me the same bridge twice,
Short from the shore, long from above — same bridge, different eyes.
I sketch both and label where I stood, not which one's a lie,
Position bends the picture, doesn't make either one a lie I tell.
At camp we set the tent on ground that's already flat,
Telescope stays caged till the tripod feet lock like that.
I put the mug on the low table, careful, off the edge,
Clip the notebook shut so wind can't tear the pledge.
First star climbs, thin cloud drags right over its spark,
I wanted the whole sky at once; he says write the dark too.
Pale sky, one bright point, breeze coming off the water,
The page already works — it holds what we saw, not what we chased after.

Chorus

Weight on the map, don't let the wind pick the story,
Write down what you saw before you chase the glory.
The mug holds the page still so nothing blows away,
We'll name what it means when the sky has had its say.

Verse 2

Dark comes down, I lower the lantern, shield it toward the table,
Small warm circle finds the mug — enough light, nothing extra.
Beyond that ring the ridge and sky start pulling apart,
Papa Bear waits for full dark before we even start.
We trade turns at the eyepiece, I call the bright point plain,
He asks what changed since last look, not what it's supposed to mean.
Cloud's moved, harbor lights sharpened, the pale band's gone from the ridge,
Small shifts matter more than guesses built on a flimsy bridge.
Then a streak cuts the sky while we're both looking up,
Too fast to draw whole — we catch direction, time, and that's enough.
Could call it a miracle, could build a whole grand claim,
Instead we write: lasted a moment, uncertain, no name.
The notebook leaves room for not knowing, doesn't force a verdict,
A quick glimpse stays a quick glimpse — we don't inflate what we've witnessed.
For a while neither of us talks, the quiet's doing its job,
I warm my hands on the mug and write: the wait gave us this view.

Chorus

Weight on the map, don't let the wind pick the story,
A streak crossed the sky — we wrote the moment, not the glory.
The mug holds the page still so nothing blows away,
We named what we saw, let the meaning come later that day.

Verse 3

Morning light, I count what's on the table before we pack,
Mug, notebook, clip, lantern, case — nothing left to come back for.
Papa Bear checks the site for scraps, loose gear, any trace,
We leave Harborlight Camp as clean as the first open space.
Same marked path home, footbridge waits at the overlook again,
Third light, third angle, I add a third small sketch below.
Nothing in the old drawings needed erasing, needed a fix,
Together the three views show how light and where you stand mix.
Four pages of notes, three sketches of one patient bridge,
No grand claim about the streak, no legend built on a ridge.
Just one habit carried home heavier than the pack we bore:
name the evidence first, let the meaning come clear.
Back at the window the teal mug returns beside the fold,
Same weight, same spot, but the hand that set it down knows more.
The trail didn't change, the bridge didn't move — we did,
we learned to let the seeing come before the verdict.

Bridge

Notebook, page one, Papa Bear's question in my own hand:
'What changed since the last look?' — not what did it mean, understand.
Page four, same question, different sky, same steady answer:
name it, date it, time it — let the meaning come after.

Chorus

Weight on the map used to mean just hold the page still,
Now it means hold off deciding till you've looked your fill.
The mug's back by the window, same spot, steadier hand,
We describe what we see first — the meaning can wait, understand.
