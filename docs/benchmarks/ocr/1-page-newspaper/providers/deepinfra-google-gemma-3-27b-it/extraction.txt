POETRY.

The Crusader's Return.

I saw my dream in outline clad,
A warrior bold, with anxious tread,
I stood once more by my own hearth fire,
And felt the warmth of the old desire.
No armor bright, no helmet shone,
But simple garb, and face well known.
He brought no trophies of the field,
But in his eyes a tale revealed.
He spoke not of the battles won,
Nor of the victories, hard begun,
But of the calm, sweet, tranquil grace,
That lingered in his journey's place.
He came to rest, his work was done,
My Crusader home, my own, my one.

MRS. EMILY JESSMORE.
