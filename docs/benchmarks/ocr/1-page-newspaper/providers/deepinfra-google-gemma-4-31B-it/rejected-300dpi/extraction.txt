The Crowder's Patience.
No more the winds of winter blow,
I hear my town in blossom blow,
From the cold, ice, electrical storm’s power
I wake to see the bloom of flower.
Where dainty soft my aeons whistles blow
And danceth with the light white snow.
As the wind blows in the garden’s calm sweet,
The fragrance of the winter’s bloom is sweet.
The water’s flow is clear, the air is bright,
The stars are twinkling in the velvet night.
The rural home, where beauty dwells so fair,
The heart is light, the spirit’s free as air.
The heart is light and happy in the day,
And softly steals the evening’s gentle ray.
And here at last my weary head I lay,
And dream of days that vanished far away.
The days of youth, when hope was bright and clear,
And love’s sweet music whispered in my ear.
Thy hand in mine, thy loving gaze on me,
And all the world was but a dream for thee.
In thy loving bosom house and heart my stay,
And love’s sweet music whispered in my day.
Fair echoes of other love to my heart’s door,
And sweet reminders of the days of yore.
Thy voice in my ears, thy loving gaze on me,
And all the world was but a dream for thee.
Their soft whispers and love, madness and rage,
The storm of life had tossed me from age to age.
There’s not a sound that haunts my soul but thy voice,
And in thy love my lonely heart shall rejoice.
My father fell in the battle, sudden and cruel,
My mother's heart was broken, a sorrowful jewel.
I be left alone, and the beat of my father’s soul,
My guide lonely he leads me on a distant goal.
My heart is heavy, my soul is filled with woe,
And lonely I wander where the cold winds blow.
O! take thy soft hand of mercy, save my soul,
And lead me to the land where the happy souls roll.
The bond of peace and pride of my native land,
Are gone, they are gone, and I stand alone hand.
Are gone, they are gone, and I stand alone hand.
Am I gone too way where the stars of heaven shine?
And lead me to the land where the happy souls shine.
