By evening the same bench faced a different harbor. The boats were quiet, the tower windows glowed, and a narrow path of light reached almost to Mira's shoes. She opened the notebook to the blank page she had saved that morning.

Papa Bear set the teal mug beside the map. A gull called from somewhere beyond the pier. They could hear it clearly, but neither of them could see it. Mira wrote the sound in the margin instead of drawing an imaginary bird.

For their last observation, they compared the outline of the tower with its reflection. The stone edges stayed sharp while the reflected edges trembled. Papa Bear turned the mug so its compass badge pointed along the pier. Mira arranged three versions of the same tower in her final sketch: the building, the reflection, and her first pencil drawing from that morning.

Mira closed the notebook after writing one final sentence. A useful record keeps what was seen separate from what was guessed. They packed the map, lifted the mug, and walked home along the lit pier.
