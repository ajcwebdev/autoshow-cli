# Episode 0: Examples

**USS ACAMPO**

## Registry Check

**INT. USS ACAMPO – ENGINEERING BAY – DAY**

The bay hums with the low drone of idle machinery. PADDY is half inside an open access panel, only his boots and a stream of muttering visible. BISHOP stands nearby with a datapad, unimpressed. A cracked display above them blinks ERROR: MODEL CATALOG OUT OF SYNC.

**PADDY**
Almost got it. The old parts list and the real parts list stopped agreeing with each other about a week ago.

**BISHOP**
(dryly)
So we have been ordering bolts that do not exist.

**PADDY**
Pretty much. Two catalogs, one ship. Classic mistake.

Paddy slides out from the panel, wipes his hands, and nods at the blinking display.

**PADDY**
Rip out the duplicate. Point everything at the one real list. Then it can never drift again.

GEEBEE hurries in, holding a second, contradictory datapad.

**GEEBEE**
Wait, before you delete anything — which list is the real one?

**BISHOP**
The one the rest of the ship already trusts.

**GEEBEE**
(relieved)
Good. Then I can finally throw this one out.

GeeBee tosses the spare datapad into the recycler. The display flickers, clears, and settles to a calm green: CATALOG: SINGLE SOURCE OK.

**PADDY**
CHAT, confirm we are reading from one catalog now.

CHAT's hologram blinks into existence beside Paddy, far too cheerful for the hour.

**CHAT**
Of course! Every system is now pointed at the central catalog. As a bonus, I can suddenly see eleven suppliers I was not allowed to use before. Shall I order something from each, for science?

**BISHOP**
No.

**CHAT**
Understood! Logging that as "no, for science."

Paddy grins, closes the panel, and the machinery spins back up to full power.
