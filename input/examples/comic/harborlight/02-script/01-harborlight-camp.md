# Episode 2: Harborlight

## Scene 1: The Lantern Path

**EXT. HARBORLIGHT CAMP - DUSK**

[Panel 1: A wide view establishes Papa Bear on the left and Mira on the right of the stone fire ring.]

Papa Bear and Mira stand beside the stone fire ring. The fixed timber bench is behind them. Papa Bear holds a small unlit lantern. Papa Bear is an adult human guide whose nickname is not an animal description.

**PAPA BEAR**
Lantern ready?

[Panel 2: A medium two-shot keeps the same left-right order.]

Mira lifts the lantern between them without changing position.

**MIRA**
Ready. I checked the wick.

[Panel 3: A closer two-shot keeps both adults visible and the bench behind them.]

Papa Bear points toward the path beyond the bench. Both remain beside the fire ring.

**PAPA BEAR**
Then let us mark the path home.

[Panel 4: A wide view returns to the same side of the pair.]

Mira holds the now-lit lantern. Papa Bear watches the path. Neither changes their place beside the fire ring.

**MIRA**
A light for whoever comes next.
